# Code Of Conduct

PyChatter aims to be a practical, welcoming project for learning and building.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections.
- Keep criticism focused on the work.
- Help keep issues and pull requests readable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Sharing private information without permission
- Spam or intentionally disruptive comments

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block users who repeatedly ignore these expectations.
